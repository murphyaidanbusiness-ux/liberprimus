Rejection switches on sharply between 40% and 60%, not gradually. Below that
the per-window phase -- a FITTED parameter, one of five per window -- flatters
a wrong key as much as the true one, and it pays off only once the base_0
assignment is well determined. So the useful first stage is about 1,100 runes,
not the 100 a really cheap bound would want.

Two stages, then: score every key at 60% and keep what clears the wrong-key
mean by 3 SD, which the planted key beats by 5.5; full-score the survivors.
That costs about 0.6 of the full score per key rather than 1.0.
