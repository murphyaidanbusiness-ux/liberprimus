## Open item: RuntimeWarnings in the lane's run (not in RESULTS.md)

`agents/laneY_section_reset/logs/run_all.log.err` holds 16 RuntimeWarning lines from `ylib.py`: divide by zero in `np.log`
(line 129, `loglik`), invalid divide in the forward pass (line 130, `fa[n] = a / s`), in the backward pass (line 136,
`be /= be.sum`) and in the posterior (line 138, `post[n] = p / p.sum`). The log does not say which tasks or candidates raised them.

- No recorded score or quadgram is non-finite (122 of 122 checked), and our replay (check 4) raised no warning.
- A zero normaliser makes the posteriors NaN. The tied fit then decodes phases with `argmax`, which returns an arbitrary
  state for NaN rows, so a candidate can be scored silently on wrong phases. A candidate that is the true restart key could
  therefore score low without any error.
- Not checked here: which tasks and candidates were affected, and whether the real LP2 best fit (or a null) depends on them.
  Checking that needs the lane's code outside check 4, which the brief does not allow.

Request to the orchestrator: have the lane record the tasks and candidates that raise these warnings, and show that the
real-LP2 best fit in both segmentations does not depend on them, before the NULL is reported as settled.

## What the verdict does and does not cover

at each chapter or each page, with the detector sensitive to restarts (plants at 0.107 to 0.311 against nulls at most 0.026).
Not covered by our checks: space skips 1-4, different start dials per segment, and the warnings item above. The lane's own
"not covered" list (independent keys per segment, other word units, the clock-dependent form) stands.
