## Differences and what they mean

- No lane number differs. The verdict is unchanged.
- LP2 "near 0" under the DM statistic (check 5) was our expectation, and it was wrong. With 145 groups of about 90
  runes and 29 symbols each, a grouping with no signal scores about -0.10 per rune, so uniform noise gives -0.0994.
  The number to trust for LP2 is the lane's own null comparison (rows 4-10), which we reproduce. Lesson: judge a
  per-rune DM score against nulls and uniform noise, not against zero.
- The lane's z uses the sample sd (+1.46); the population sd gives +1.48. Both round near the lane's value; no change.
- Checks 5 and 6 use the same English segment (char 900000) with different keys and seeds, as the orchestrator asked.

## What the verdict does and does not cover

Covered (reproduced here): one continuous key over 12,956 runes, any keyed alphabet (check 1 shows the grouping is a
fixed substitution for random K), both variants, space skips 0-4 (lane rows 12-14). Not covered by this check, and
still as the lane states: 6,000- and 3,000-rune spans (detection not calibrated), word units other than LP2's
separators, and the clock-dependent variant (lane W). The stage-2 misses (hold plants 5 and 9; skips 1-4) do not
bear on the verdict, which rests on stage-1 detection, and they are reproduced as stated.
