## Differences and their causes

1. **Containment (section 3, "every span of 1.5 T").** Windows start at the first word at or after s = k T/2 and end at the
   first word at or after s + T, so a window can start a few runes after s and run up to 5 runes past s + T. A span that
   begins one rune after a window's start loses that window. Worst cases: T 4,000 from rune 6,003 (window 4 is 8,001-12,006,
   window 3 starts at 6,002), need 6,003 runes; T 6,000 from rune 3,001 (window 1 starts at 3,000), need 9,005 runes.
   **Trust ours** (exact, from the lane's own layout). The verdict's threshold is therefore 6,003 (not 6,000) and 9,005
   (not 9,000) consecutive runes. This does not change the verdict.
2. **Coincidence rate range.** The 0.051 low end matches chapter 14 only (0.0508). Chapter 8 plant 1 (hold) is 168/3,481 =
   0.0483. **Trust ours** (all six plants); the lane should say "0.048 to 0.057 over six plants, 0.051 to 0.056 on chapter 14".
   The evidence numbers quoted for both chapters reproduce.
3. **"Lane X catches 1 of 4 plants" on the largest chapter (section 1 summary).** No record in `out/` supports it. The probe
   has 3 plants per chapter, and `out/probe.jsonl` gives 2 of 3 above the null max on chapter 14 (and 2 of 3 on chapter 8,
   0 of 3 on chapter 15). Pooled, 4 of 9 plants beat their 5-null maximum, against 1.5 expected by chance (binomial
   P(>= 4) = 0.05 under exchangeability). That is weak, not a finding, and no probe plant decrypts; "untested for lack of
   power" still stands. **Trust the out file.** If the "1 of 4" came from an earlier run, that run is not in `out/`.
4. **Window 3 margin cut.** The table's window 3 is words [1,359, 2,266), 3,999 runes (6,002-10,001). The follow-up's
   "shift 0" is words [1,359, 2,267), 4,003 runes, one word longer, and its 100 nulls are cut to that span. So "fell to
   z +1.53" compares two cuts: +2.04 (3,999 runes, 20 nulls) and +1.53 (4,003 runes, 100 nulls). The text does say
   "as re-cut", but the 3,999-rune window was not re-tested against 100 nulls. Neither z approaches 5.
5. **Replay decryption (not a lane number).** See the replay section; the lane's stage 2 does not decrypt the fresh plant.

## Does the verdict stand?

- **"NULL for any key over 6,000+ consecutive runes": the not-detected result stands, with two corrections.**
  (a) The threshold is 6,003 runes for T 4,000 windows and 9,005 for T 6,000 windows (difference 1).
  (b) Power is lower than "excluded" implies. Our reproduction of the plant counts gives 19/24 per window at T 4,000
  (Wilson 95%: 0.60 to 0.91) and 15/16 at T 6,000 (0.72 to 0.99). A key of 6,003 to about 9,000 runes always contains a
  T 4,000 window, but a T 6,000 window only for some placements, so for those keys the T 4,000 figure applies. "Excluded"
  should read "not detected, with per-window power of roughly 60 to 90 percent". The same applies to "a book in two parts
  with their own keys is excluded": the larger part (at least 6,478 runes) contains one or two T 4,000 windows depending on
  where it sits, so that conclusion carries the same per-window power.
- **"Single chapters untested for lack of power": stands.** No LP2 chapter run was made; the probe's 4 of 9 above-max
  plants are weak evidence and the lane's summary sentence about them has no record. Fix the "1 of 4" sentence.
- **Window 3 (z +2.04, +1.53 on a different cut): not a lead.** It sits at the top of 21 scores (0 of 20 nulls above) and
  one window above all 20 nulls among six windows is expected about a quarter of the time (1 - (20/21)^6 = 0.25).
- **Lead check (CLAUDE.md):** no LP2 number is at z >= 5. Nothing was emailed or notified; subagents report only.
