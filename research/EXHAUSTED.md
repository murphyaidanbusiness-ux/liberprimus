# What the community has already ruled out on LP2, and how solidly

Date: 2026-10-18. Job: `jobs/exhaustion-census/`. Four Haiku parts extracted the rows; the orchestrator (claude-opus-5-5)
then judged every row. Full table: `research/census/merged.md`, generated by `research/census/merge.py` from the
verdicts in `research/census/judgments.tsv` (written by `write_judgments.py`, which records each decision and its reason).
Parts: `research/census/dukotah.md`, `repos.md` (aldegonde, Leo-Y-Zhang, TheronEagle), `aldegonde2.md` (the 21 aldegonde
files the first pass skipped, plus Theron's `ruled-out.md`) and `web.md` (public clearweb sources not in our clones).

## Bottom line

- **232 ruled-out methods, each read from its source and judged.** 130 are dead by the LP2 fingerprint whatever the key,
  so how weakly they were searched does not matter. Another 21 were searched in full or refuted by a direct test.
- **Most of the rest is not a finite search.** 21 are open-ended (seed dictionaries, key texts, pads, unbounded
  classes), which no search can exhaust; 34 are about images, the payload or hashes, not the rune stream; 20 are
  statistical probes of a mechanism.
- **Six are partly tested, consistent with the fingerprint, and finite.** Five are walk/dodge variants, the one family
  the constraints leave. None is a cheap, high-hope reopen (the shortlist and Hope scores are below).
- **Two corrections to our own record came out of it.** C2c is Oosterhof's test first, and the "hold" variant of the
  odometer dodge is covered by tiers A and B (*What the census changed*).
- **The judging pass mattered.** The agents flagged 99 reopen candidates. Judging every row re-graded 27 (26 constraint
  cells to dead, one strength) and moved the rest into buckets where a re-test would not be a finite search.

## How each row was graded

- **Strength (the agents):** A = exhaustive over a stated space and a planted key recovered by the same search;
  B = exhaustive, no control; C = a sample or partial space; D = "tried, nothing" with no stated space.
- **Constraint (the agents, then judged):** `dead C<n>` when the method cannot produce LP2's fingerprint
  (`toolbox/CONSTRAINTS.md`), else `consistent` or `unclear`.
- **Bucket (the orchestrator, one per row):** dead by a constraint; searched in full; refuted by a direct test;
  structure probe; open-ended; off-stream; partial and finite (the reopen list).

Read down the columns: every classical family (periodic, transposition, substitution, prime streams, autokeys,
running keys) is closed by the fingerprint itself, not by a failed sweep. The open-ended keystream rows (Dukotah's seed
dictionaries, KDFs, pads) survive only as *filtered* keystreams (a stream with a skip rule on top), can never be
finished, and leave the within-word lag-5 echo unexplained (a word-blind stream does not predict it; AX-59, soft). The
walk/dodge family is the only one with finite, partly searched pieces.

## What the census changed

1. **C2c is an extension of Oosterhof's test, not a new constraint.** `dl/aldegonde_clone/hypotheses/doublet-preventer-clock-drift.md`
   already gives LP2's mod-5 doublet phase counts (18, 21, 16, 11, 20; chi-square 3.7; flat at every space skip 0-4) and
   uses them to rule out drift-free preventers. We found the same counts independently in lane S and missed the file.
   What we add is moduli 2-8, the within-page index, and the totient and dynamic-substitution variants it excludes.
   Corrected in `toolbox/CONSTRAINTS.md`, `REPORT_ROUND2.md` and the community draft.
2. **The odometer dodge's "hold" variant is covered by tiers A and B.** The community's scorer self-test plants it, but
   the gate, the community sweep and our tiers planted "advance" only. It passes the gate (marginal C3, 1 of 12 seeds),
   and lane O's sweep scorer recovers 18 of 20 planted "hold" keyword keys at rank 1 (median z +22.8). So the tier A+B
   nulls exclude it at roughly the same power as "advance". See `agents/laneO_quagmire_dodge/RESULTS.md` section 11.
3. **Lane W's finding stands.** The community's own check of a clock-form key (`experiments/dodge_scorer_check.py`)
   tested the older `walk_score_kernel`, not `dodge_aware_scorer`, so nobody had scored `quagmire_dodge.encipher` with
   the scorer that the hypothesis file credits.

## Limits of the census

- Web: Reddit refused the fetch tool, the Uncovering Cicada wiki returned 402, and the DEF CON 31 captions and one wiki
  failed; WebFetch returns a model summary, so web rows are paraphrase. No Discord exports, onions or archives were read.
- Some long files were read by their status and verdict sections only (listed in each part's Sources checked).
- Dukotah's in-flight items (R25 tail, R26-A/B, R27 S2, R28-L4) are not rows.
- The judging pass checked each row's verdict against `toolbox/CONSTRAINTS.md` and, where needed, the source; it did not
  re-open every source. Four rows were spot-checked against their sources and matched.
