# Exhaustion census, part: aldegonde2 (AX-) and Theron ruled-out (TX-)

## Bottom line
- 78 rows: 66 AX from 21 aldegonde hypothesis files, 12 TX from Theron's `ruled-out.md`. Strength A 0, B 27, C 30, D 21. 40 rows are dead by a named constraint; 28 are reopen candidates.
- No row is strength A: none of these sources plants a key and recovers it over a stated space, so every B and C row is ruled out only within its sampled space.
- The arithmetic and small-state walk variants (order-5 steps from arithmetic maps, arithmetic sigma, fixed-phase blocks, phase counters, powers of one permutation, the 5-ring cylinder) are dead on C1, C2, C2c, C3 or C5. The open cells are the ones the sources call unresolved: inner layers with 6 or more runes of context, keyword-grid state returns, and the DJU-BEI repeat in fitted walk keys.
- Controls are mostly absent in these files: 4 rows have control y, 1 has control n (TX-02, its positive control peaked at the wrong period), and the rest are unclear. 14 rows have a shuffled or simulated null (y); where a file gives only an analytic bound or a point figure, the null is n.
